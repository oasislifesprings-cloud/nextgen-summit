About the exhibition

meet the
next generation.

NextGen Summit is a one-day experience for students and young adults to grow, connect, and see what’s possible. On Friday, October 30, at the Gordon Center for Performing Arts in Maryland, it bridges the gap between rising entrepreneurs and established industry leaders.

Alongside keynotes, B2B networking and the flagship $10,000 Live Pitch Competition, a high-impact exhibition lobby puts your brand directly in front of decision-makers, prospective clients, top youth talent and regional media.
