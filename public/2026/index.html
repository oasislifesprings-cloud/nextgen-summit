The recap

NextGen 2026 was a one-day experience for students and young adults to grow, connect, and see what’s possible for their future.

Nextgen thinkers, builders, leaders, creators, changemakers. nextgen thinkers.

The Experience

The experience

  1. think bigger.

    Talks from people already doing the work, and honest advice on purpose and career.

  2. meet people.

    High school, college and early career were all in one room. People came with friends, or came solo.

  3. find what's next.

    People left with a clearer next move, and people to take it with.
