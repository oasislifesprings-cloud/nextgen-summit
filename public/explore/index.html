Explore NextGen

You can.

Two words we want every person in the room to believe about themselves by the time they leave.

Most people do not lack ambition. They lack access: to the room, to the person who has done it, to the honest answer about what comes next.

NextGen Summit is a one-day experience built to close that gap. It brings together people, ideas and opportunities so that what felt out of reach starts to look like a plan.

Come for fresh perspective. Leave with direction.

Road

Four things shape every part of the experience. We call them ROAD.

  1. RRelationships

    The people you meet are the point, not the programming around them.

  2. OOpportunity

    In 2026, ten founders pitched live for a share of $10,000. Others found the opening they were looking for.

  3. AAccess

    Candid conversations with people who have already walked it, in the same room as you.

  4. DDirection

    You leave knowing your next move, not just feeling good about the day.

Questions

FAQ

What is NextGen?

A one-day experience for students and young adults to grow, connect, and see what’s possible for their future.

Who is it for?

Young people ages 17–29: high school students, college students and young professionals.

Can I come alone?

Yes. You don’t need to bring a friend or a group. Plenty of people meet for the first time.

When and where is NextGen 2027?

The date and the city are still to be announced. Join the 2027 waitlist and pick your city to hear first.

Where was NextGen 2026?

Friday, October 30, 2026, at the Gordon Center for Performing Arts in Owings Mills, Maryland. See NextGen 2026.

Was there a pitch competition?

Yes. In 2026, twenty founders age 29 and under reached the semifinals, and ten pitched live on October 30 for a share of $10,000.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. Write to hello@nextgensummit.us and tell us a little about you.

Can an organization partner with NextGen?

Yes. Write to hello@nextgensummit.us.
