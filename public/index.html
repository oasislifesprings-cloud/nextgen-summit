01What is Next Gen

Nextgen thinkers, builders, leaders, creators, changemakers. nextgen thinkers.

A free one-day experience for students and young adults to grow, connect, and see what's possible for their future.

02The Experience

The experience

  1. think bigger.

    Talks from people already doing the work, plus honest advice on purpose and career.

  2. meet people.

    Students from campuses across Maryland in one room. Come with friends, or come solo.

  3. find what's next.

    Leave with a clearer next move, and people to take it with.

Questions first?

FAQ

good questions.

When is Next Gen?

Saturday, October 31, 2–8 PM.

Where is Next Gen?

Joseph Meyerhoff Symphony Hall, 1212 Cathedral Street, Baltimore, Maryland.

Who can attend?

College students, emerging young adults, and older high-school students. Students from all campuses are welcome.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Next Gen Summit is free to attend. You only need to register so we know you are coming.

Do I need to register?

Yes, and it is free. Register here to save your spot. It takes about a minute.

Can I come alone?

Yes. You don't need to bring a friend or a group. Plenty of people will be meeting for the first time.

What should I wear?

To be confirmed. We'll share it before the day.

How do I get there?

Take the Light Rail to Cultural Center. The hall is right across Park Avenue. The Metro stop at State Center also works. Parking details are to be confirmed.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. and tell us a little about you.

Can an organization partner with Next Gen?

Yes. and choose Partner.
